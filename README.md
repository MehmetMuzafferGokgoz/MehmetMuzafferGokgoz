### About

I am a third-year Software Engineering student at Istanbul Health and Technology University, working at the intersection of deep learning and embedded hardware. My work focuses on real-time perception on edge devices, from training object detection models to deploying them on resource-constrained platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson Nano.

I am particularly interested in robotics perception, defense technologies and low-level systems.
