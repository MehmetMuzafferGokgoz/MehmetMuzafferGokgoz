### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a third-year **Software Engineering** student at Istanbul Health and Technology University, working at the intersection of **deep learning** and **embedded hardware**. I train object detection models and deploy them on resource-constrained platforms for real-time perception.

- 🔭 &nbsp;Focus: **Computer Vision · Deep Learning · Embedded Systems · Robotics Perception**
- 🧪 &nbsp;Research: driver drowsiness detection with deep learning on **NVIDIA Jetson Nano**
- 📱 &nbsp;Shipped: **Flag Quiz**, a Flutter app live on Google Play in 22 languages
- 🛰️ &nbsp;Interested in: defense technologies, low-level systems and mobile apps
- 📍 &nbsp;Based in **Istanbul, Türkiye**

### 📄 Research & Publications

- **Peer-reviewed journal article** — *International Journal of 3D Printing Technologies and Digital Industry (IJ3DPTDI)*, March 2026<br/>
  Deep learning-based driver fatigue and drowsiness detection. [View on DergiPark →](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/@mehmet-muzaffer-gokgoz)
- **TÜBİTAK 2209-A Undergraduate Research Project** — 2023/2 term, completed<br/>
  Benchmarking YOLO models for real-time drowsiness detection and emotion analysis on NVIDIA Jetson Nano.
